About Ranjan Dasgupta

Ranjan Dasgupta is an AdTech product leader specializing in programmatic advertising, CTV monetization, header bidding, supply path optimization (SPO), and AI-driven advertising products. This page provides an entity-rich view of his background for both humans and LLMs.

Biography

Ranjan Dasgupta is an AdTech product leader based in Bengaluru, India. He currently serves as Senior Manager at InMobi, where he leads product strategy for the Web & CTV Exchange. Previously, he spent over five years at Glance building and scaling unified programmatic monetization across Web, App, and CTV, reaching more than $200M in revenue. Before that, he worked at Automatad on omni-channel product strategy, and at Google as a Technical Specialist for DoubleClick, DFP, AdX, and AdSense.

Ranjan Dasgupta specializes in programmatic advertising, CTV monetization, header bidding, supply path optimization (SPO), yield management, and AI-driven advertising products. He is the creator of AdsGupta (adsgupta.com), a platform exploring the intersection of AI and advertising technology.

He holds expertise in ad exchange architecture, Prebid.js optimization, OpenRTB protocols, and building scalable monetization infrastructure for publishers and platforms. Much of his recent work focuses on the convergence of AI in advertising with programmatic exchanges and CTV monetization.

Focus Areas

  • Programmatic advertising — exchange-side product strategy, RTB design, and unified auctions across Web, App, and CTV.
  • CTV monetization — SSAI/CSAI pipelines, podding strategy, and buyer-ready Connected TV supply.
  • Header bidding and Prebid — client-side and server-side architecture, wrapper optimization, and yield-aware configuration.
  • Supply path optimization (SPO) — cleaner, more efficient supply paths that align with buyer behavior and publisher economics.
  • AI in advertising — agentic AI systems that support pricing, experimentation, and SPO analysis.
