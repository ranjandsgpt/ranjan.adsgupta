How the AdsGupta Exchange Works

When a publisher's page loads, the AdsGupta Exchange receives an ad request and constructs an OpenRTB 2.6 bid request describing the ad opportunity — the slot size, the page context, the user's device, and the floor price. This bid request is sent to multiple demand-side platforms (DSPs) simultaneously, mirroring how modern programmatic advertising strategy is executed across exchanges.

In parallel, each DSP decides whether to bid based on its campaigns, targeting rules, and budgets. Some respond with bids, some respond with "no bid", and some may timeout if the exchange-enforced deadline is too aggressive. The AdsGupta Exchange enforces a strict timeout budget and then runs a first-price auction on the bids it received. This is similar to how header bidding and unified auctions work in practice, as described on the header bidding and Prebid optimization page.

The highest valid bid that meets or exceeds the floor price wins the auction. The exchange records the clearing price, the bid landscape, and latency metrics for analytics and yield optimization for publishers. It then returns the winning creative markup to the publisher page, fires the DSP's win notice URL, and logs the impression and any subsequent click events.

Along the way, quality and compliance checks ensure that creatives meet policy requirements, advertiser domains respect publisher block lists, and supply paths align with supply path optimization (SPO) strategy. The result is a transparent, efficient auction that serves both publishers and buyers.

Frequently Asked Questions about Ad Exchange Demos

What is an ad exchange?
An ad exchange is a technology platform that facilitates the buying and selling of digital advertising inventory through real-time auctions. The AdsGupta Exchange demo on this page shows how bid requests and bid responses flow through a first-price auction, built and operated by Ranjan Dasgupta.
What is OpenRTB?
OpenRTB (Open Real-Time Bidding) is the industry-standard protocol maintained by the IAB Tech Lab that defines how ad exchanges communicate with demand-side platforms. The AdsGupta Exchange demo uses OpenRTB 2.6-style objects to illustrate real-world programmatic transactions.
How fast does a real ad auction run?
A production ad auction typically completes in under 200 milliseconds from the moment the ad tag fires to the creative being returned. This demo simulates that behavior with controlled delays and timeouts so you can see an exchange's timing constraints in action.
